## Setting up LinkSync

LinkSync is designed to run from your iPhone's Action Button or any iOS Shortcut. After installing:

1. Open LinkSync once to complete the brief onboarding.
2. Follow the on-screen instructions to add LinkSync to your Action Button (**Settings > Action Button > Shortcut > LinkSync**) or set up a custom Shortcut.
3. Copy a music link from Apple Music or Spotify.
4. Press your Action Button (or run the Shortcut) — the converted link will open automatically.

Once configured, you never need to open the LinkSync app again. The conversion happens entirely in the background.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why didn't my link convert?

LinkSync only converts links from the official Apple Music and Spotify catalogs. If a song is region-restricted, not available on the target service, or the link format is unsupported, the conversion may fail. Try:

- Confirming the source link still works in its original app
- Checking that the song is available on the target service in your region
- Updating LinkSync to the latest version

### Do I need an Apple Music or Spotify account?

No. LinkSync uses public lookup endpoints to find matching tracks. You don't need to sign in to either service to use LinkSync — although you'll need the corresponding app installed to actually play the converted link.

### Does LinkSync collect my data?

The free app does not: it creates no account, collects no personal information, and never tracks usage. The optional **Premium** subscription is the only part that stores data — when you sign in with Apple, it keeps a private history of the links people send you, tied to your own account and visible only to you. You can delete your account and all of that data anytime from **Settings → Account → Delete account**. See our [Privacy Policy](https://albertogparedes.github.io/linksync-privacy/) for full details.

### What platforms does LinkSync support?

LinkSync is currently available on iOS. Android support is not currently planned.

### Is LinkSync free?

Yes — converting links is free forever, with no ads. LinkSync also offers an optional **Premium** subscription that adds a private, synced history of the links people send you. Premium is entirely optional; you never need it to convert links. You can manage or cancel a subscription anytime in your App Store or Google Play account settings.
